Grandma Rosa's Lasagna

Serves 8 | Prep 45 min | Bake 50 min at 375F

1 lb ground beef, 1 lb Italian sausage
2 jars (24 oz) marinara or 6 cups homemade sauce
16 oz ricotta, 1 egg, 1/2 cup parmesan, 1 lb mozzarella
12 lasagna noodles, fresh basil

Brown the meats, drain. Simmer with sauce 20 min.
Mix ricotta, egg, parmesan, pinch of nutmeg (the secret!).
Layer: sauce, noodles, ricotta mix, mozzarella. Repeat x3.
Cover with foil, bake 35 min. Uncover, bake 15 more.
REST 15 MINUTES before cutting or it slides everywhere.
